ABOUT OUR COMPANY

A Force Security &
Allied Services.

A Force Security & Allied Services is an authorized, registered and licensed security agency, registered and approved under PSARA of the Government of Maharashtra.

AFS Security and Allied Services is a one-point solution for protection and security services. The company has market experience and is committed to understanding client needs and requirements.

We have a well-organized and professional computerized setup with the requisite infrastructure, through which we provide effective and unique services. Our manpower staff are generally drawn from different Indian states and are periodically rotated.

Established on 23 January 2020 · years of service

🎯 Our Aim

To relieve management of all workmen and allied challenges — from sourcing skilled labour and ensuring statutory compliance to providing effective supervision for smooth, uninterrupted productivity.

🚀 Our Mission

To protect the life and property of our customers across varied business activities, delivering high-quality services that consistently meet and exceed expectations.
